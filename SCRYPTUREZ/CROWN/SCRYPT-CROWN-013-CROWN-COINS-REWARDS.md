# SCRYPT-CROWN-013 — CROWN COINS PROMOTIONAL REWARDS

Status: ACTIVE / DESIGN; NOT LIVE
Version: 1.0
Environment: Crown Network / Founding Members / Store

## Canon
- Crown Coins are **non-cash promotional rewards**, not legal tender, cryptocurrency, investment products, or stored cash value.
- Crown Coins have **no promised monetary value**, cash-out, or guaranteed redemption equivalent in USD.
- Their purpose is rewarding community participation and redeeming eligible AWE-owned goods or perks through future Crown Store terms.
- Neither awarding coins nor operating a store is live or authorized by this design document alone.

## Example Founding-Member Campaign (not a live offer)
- Owner concept: 5,000 promotional Crown Coins for each qualifying verified founding signup.
- Possible catalog: T-shirt for 5,000 coins; sticker sheet for 1,000 coins.
- These are illustration quantities; inventory, availability, eligibility, account limits, expiration (if any), shipping, taxes, and redemption terms must be published before launch.
- No account should receive an award until a campaign has been explicitly approved and activated.

## Registration / Attribution
- Founder eligibility must require successfully verified email identity, not merely a signup attempt.
- Referral attribution (example: \`x-tha-god\`) is an organizer/fan analytics attribute; it is not proof of association or authorization.
- The Crown Door discovery experience remains distinct from a shareable invite link.
- Promotional marketing communications require appropriate, separately recorded consent; transactional verification email is not a marketing opt-in.

## Future Redemption Safety
- Every award and redemption posts append-only entries in \`loyalty_ledger\`.
- Redemption must be atomic with balance checking; avoid negative balances, double spending, and retry duplication.
- Never derive a cash value or customer entitlement solely from a displayed coin balance.
- Future program rules must cover inventory limits, transferability, reversals/refunds, tax/shipping treatment, fraud, and legal compliance.

## Launch Sequence
1. Confirm Preview email verification end to end.
2. Establish verified founder cutoff and campaign eligibility rules.
3. Add privacy notice, terms, anti-abuse, and referral reporting.
4. Approve coin allocation and store redemption catalog.
5. Publish promotional terms, then activate coins and store separately.
