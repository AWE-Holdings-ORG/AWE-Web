## Product Position
CYPHERZ is a public-facing culture/community application. It is not a CROWD-only app and does not teach users how to find or join Crown Network.

## Identity
CYPHERZ users may have a normal CYPHERZ identity.
An existing Crown member may later link CYPHERZ to an existing AW ID/Crown Identity.
CYPHERZ never receives or stores a member PCK as part of the linking flow.

## Access Relationship
CYPHERZ natively participates in PUBLIC, HOUSE, and UNLOCK experiences.
CROWN and VAULT may appear as unexplained locked/encrypted identifiers without a direct Crown-access tutorial.
